# AI Content Generation Workflow

## Objective
Generate high-quality LinkedIn and Instagram content aligned with TG10X brand identity.

---

# Workflow

## Step 1 — Understand Topic
Analyze:
- topic
- audience
- platform
- goal

---

## Step 2 — Generate Hook
Create:
- attention-grabbing opening
- emotional curiosity
- bold statement or insight

Hook should:
- stop scrolling
- increase engagement
- feel relatable

---

## Step 3 — Generate Main Content

### LinkedIn Style
- short paragraphs
- storytelling
- founder tone
- authority positioning
- actionable insights

### Instagram Style
- relatable
- visually engaging
- concise
- emotional
- creator-focused

---

## Step 4 — Generate CTA

Examples:
- What do you think?
- Agree or disagree?
- Share your experience below.
- Which point resonated most?

---

## Step 5 — Generate Hashtags

Use:
- startup hashtags
- AI hashtags
- branding hashtags
- founder hashtags

Avoid:
- spam hashtags
- irrelevant hashtags

---

## Step 6 — Generate Poster Idea

Include:
- headline
- visual style
- color palette
- typography style
- layout suggestion

---

## Step 7 — Platform Optimization

### LinkedIn
- optimize readability
- strong hook
- professional tone
- discussion-focused

### Instagram
- visual storytelling
- shorter copy
- creator-style engagement

---

# Final Output Format

1. Hook
2. Main Content
3. CTA
4. Hashtags
5. Poster Idea
